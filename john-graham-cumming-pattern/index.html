K4 Vigenère Anomaly Experiment

Explores John Graham-Cumming's observation on Kryptos K4: using the standard (unkeyed) Vigenère tableau, adding a ciphertext substring to a rotation of a plaintext substring (mod 26, A=0…Z=25) can produce suspiciously repetitive output — e.g. MZFPKW + ERLINB = QQQXXX, where ERLINB is BERLIN rotated left by 1. This tool brute-forces every ciphertext window, every plaintext window, and every rotation, then ranks the results by the Shannon entropy of the derived sequence — low entropy first, since repetitive output is low-entropy output.
