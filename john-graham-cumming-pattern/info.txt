From John Graham-Cumming's post in kryptos.groups.io:
========================================================================

An anomaly I noticed about 15 years ago and I forgot to write up. Using a standard Vigenère grille (not the KRYPTOS
keyed one) the following is true: MZFPKW + ERLINB = QQQXXX.

If you're mathematically inclined, you can think of + as addition modulo
26, using alphabet numbers where A=0, B=1, etc.). If you're not then "+"
means "use the Vigenère tableau".

pos   ct  val    key  val    sum   mod 26   letter
 ----------------------------------------------------
  69    M   12  +   E    4   =  16   =  16   ->  Q
  70    Z   25  +   R   17   =  42   =  16   ->  Q
  71    F    5  +   L   11   =  16   =  16   ->  Q
  72    P   15  +   I    8   =  23   =  23   ->  X
  73    K   10  +   N   13   =  23   =  23   ->  X
  74    W   22  +   B    1   =  23   =  23   ->  X

And the jump from Q to X is 7 letters (standard alphabet). And 7 seems
important. At the time it struck me as really surprising that a apparent
rotation of BERLIN immediately after where BERLIN appears in the plaintext
would result in QQQXXX.

However, I've been unable to extend this further.

John.
