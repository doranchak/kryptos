Cipher Generator

Manual encrypt / decrypt, and bulk cipher-sample generation, for 36 classical pen-and-paper ciphers.

Key

In fixed-length mode, the target length is the desired final ciphertext length; the generator works backward to figure out how much plaintext to select so the ciphertext comes out at that length, so ciphers that fractionate or expand text (Homophonic, ADFGX, ADFGVX roughly halve the plaintext needed) or add fixed overhead (Mirdek's 25-letter IV) get proportionally less plaintext, not a longer ciphertext. The ciphertext length never exceeds the target; a handful of ciphers can't always hit it exactly (Homophonic/ADFGX/ADFGVX only land on even targets exactly; Playfair's double-letter fillers and Hill's block padding depend on the actual letters chosen) and may come in a little under instead. In length-range mode, the generator accepts any ciphertext length within min-max (inclusive) instead of hunting for one exact value - useful for ciphers that can't land on an arbitrary exact length.
